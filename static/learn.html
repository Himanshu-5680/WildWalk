How a walk pack is made

Five steps, all open-first

1. Find spotsSerpApi searches the web; OpenStreetMap fills in if it is offline.
2. Score the walkTabPFN reads temperature, humidity and AQI.
3. Recall factsTiger Data and MongoDB Atlas add facts and your walk memory.
4. Write the guideGemma writes a short script.
5. Add voiceElevenLabs narrates it; your browser voice is the backup.
